Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

loosedraw is a free hand-drawn whiteboard. This page explains, in plain words, what data it keeps and why. The short version: your drawings stay in your browser unless you sign in, we collect only what sign-in needs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.

Without an account

You can use loosedraw without signing in. Your scenes, folders, version history and settings are saved in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

With an account

If you sign in, we store:

  • Your account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or GitHub, the name and profile picture those services share. We use these only to sign you in and to show who is signed in.
  • Your scenes and folders: so they sync across your devices. Version history stays on each device and is not uploaded.
  • Technical records: when you signed in and when scenes were last synced, to keep sync working and to protect accounts.

Only you can read your synced scenes. Access rules in our database refuse every request that is not signed in as you.

Signing in with Google or GitHub

When you choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with GitHub”, that service asks for your permission and then shares your basic profile (email address, name and picture) with us. We do not get your password and cannot see or change anything else in those accounts. Google's and GitHub's own privacy policies apply to what happens on their pages.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service, where account details and synced scenes are stored.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website. Like any web host, it may keep short-term access logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Google and GitHub, only if you sign in with them.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not sell your data.

Cookies and storage

loosedraw uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies. It keeps your scenes and settings there and, when you are signed in, the session that keeps you signed in.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a scene: move it to the trash, then delete it for good. Scenes left in the trash are deleted after 30 days.
  • Delete your account: open the account menu at the top right of the app and choose Delete account. Your account and every synced scene are deleted from our servers straight away. Scenes saved on your device stay there until you remove them.
  • Sign out: stops syncing on that device and keeps your scenes on it.

Children

loosedraw is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. For a significant change, we will also tell signed-in users in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. A dedicated email address will be listed here soon.
