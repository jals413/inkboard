loosedraw

A free online whiteboard with a hand-drawn look, for diagrams, flowcharts, wireframes and notes.

  • Hand-drawn shapes, sticky notes, pen and highlighter
  • Arrows that stay attached to shapes, with curves and right-angle elbows
  • Tables, LaTeX formulas and a library of ready-made shapes
  • Mermaid flowcharts turned into editable diagrams
  • Export to PNG and SVG, and import of Excalidraw files
  • Works offline and keeps your drawings on your device
